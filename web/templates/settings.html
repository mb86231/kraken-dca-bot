Local Admin

Username/password login for the dashboard. The password is stored only as a bcrypt hash in the secrets store (data/secrets.json, 0600). If the WEB_UI_USERNAME / WEB_UI_PASSWORD_HASH environment variables are set, they take precedence. Applies to new logins immediately; existing sessions stay valid.

Leave blank to keep the current password. Minimum 8 characters.

Authentik (OIDC)

Sign in via your Authentik SSO. Settings are stored in the secrets store (data/secrets.json, 0600) and take effect immediately unless the OIDC_* environment variables are set, which take precedence. Local login remains available as a fallback either way.
